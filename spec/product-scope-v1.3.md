# Product Scope Addendum — Architecture Version 1.3

**Status:** FROZEN

## Supported agency operating domains

MarketingOS core supports customer acquisition and audience operations through a common Goal/Workflow/Execution model.

Initial domain packs/capabilities include:

- Performance/Growth Marketing;
- Field Acquisition;
- Creator Operations.

Additional verticals may be introduced as Domain Packs without changing core authorities.

## Creator Operations

MarketingOS is capable of supporting agencies that manage creators, including creator managers, chat operations, content/growth operations, audience engagement and monetization workflows.

This does not mean MarketingOS is a provider-specific creator application. Specific creator platforms remain integrations/extensions.

## Commercial operating model

The platform supports agency-run client work and platform-mediated Human Agent work. A Goal may create governed Tasks that are executed by agency staff, platform Human Agents, AI capabilities, deterministic functions or extensions.

Bounded acquisition pilots may be used to demonstrate measurable results before a longer-term client engagement; this reuses the standard Goal → Experiment → Workflow → Execution → Evidence/Outcome lifecycle.
